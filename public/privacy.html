Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Overview

Egg Diet Tracker ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever.

Data Storage

All information you enter into the App - including meal logs, weight entries, and your start date - is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server or third party.

No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

No Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use any analytics services, advertising frameworks, or tracking technologies. We do not collect usage data, device information, or any other telemetry.

No Third-Party Services

The App does not connect to any third-party services, APIs, or servers. It works entirely offline.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all App data at any time by using the "Reset All Progress" button within the App, or by deleting the App from your device. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@eggdiettracker.com.
